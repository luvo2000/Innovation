GERMANY Education

Where Knowledge Has No Price Tag

420,000 international students. Tuition-free public universities. 76 Fraunhofer institutes. 84 Max Planck institutes. Germany's education and research ecosystem is one of the world's great undervalued assets.

Global Talent Destination

400+ Universities. Near-Zero Tuition. A Record 420,000 International Students.

Germany's public university system is one of the few in the world where academic quality and affordability are not in tension. EU students pay no tuition. Most non-EU students pay none either.

🏫

Research Infrastructure

76 Fraunhofer Institutes. 84 Max Planck Institutes. One Ecosystem.

Germany's research network is not just academic infrastructure. It is industrial leverage. Companies co-develop IP here that becomes global product.

Applied Research Leader

Europe's largest applied research organisation. Fraunhofer bridges academic discovery and industrial production — its institutes work directly with companies on contract research, from semiconductor fabrication to AI systems, solar energy, and biomedical devices. Funding Your Studies

DAAD, Erasmus, and the Most Accessible Public University System in the World

Even where tuition applies, Germany offers more scholarship pathways per student than almost any other destination. The DAAD alone funds over 140,000 scholars annually.

🏆

DAAD — German Academic Exchange Service

Germany
140,000+ Scholars/Year

The world's largest funding organisation for international academic exchange. DAAD offers scholarships for all academic levels — from undergraduate study abroad grants to postdoctoral research fellowships — across 140+ countries. Its doctoral programmes provide full funding for 4-year PhD research at German institutions.

Federal Programme
€300/month
