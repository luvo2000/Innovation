Berlin
Germany

Europe's fastest-growing founder ecosystem. Home to Factory Berlin, Axel Springer, and a deep-tech startup corridor stretching from Mitte to Kreuzberg. The city that reinvents itself every decade.

Germany

LMU and TUM campus cluster, BMW Group headquarters, Infineon Technologies, and a bleisure capital for international business travellers. Engineering culture embedded into every institution.

Germany
