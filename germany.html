Two Landscapes
🏛
Neuschwanstein
Bavaria, Germany

King Ludwig II's fairy-tale castle perched above the Bavarian Alps. One of the most visited landmarks in Europe and the inspiration behind Disney's Sleeping Beauty Castle.

⛪
Cologne Cathedral
Cologne, Germany

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and Germany's most visited monument. 632 years in the making, it remains one of the finest examples of Gothic architecture in the world.

🌅
Rhine Valley
Germany

Medieval castles, vineyard terraces, and river towns stretching 65 km along the Middle Rhine. Another UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Germany's defining natural landscapes.
