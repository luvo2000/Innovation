Academic Documentation

This campaign was developed as part of an MBA seminar paper at FOM University of Applied Sciences. The strategy draws on Anholt's Nation Branding Hexagon and Dinnie's competitive identity framework, applied to Germany's deep-tech innovation ecosystem. The full paper covers campaign objectives, target audience analysis, media planning, and instrument design.
